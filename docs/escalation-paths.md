# Escalation Paths (Draft)

Draft guide for pulling in more help when an incident outgrows the current
on-call engineer. Review before next quarter's rotation starts.

## Standard escalation ladder

1. **Primary on-call** — first responder, owns the incident.
2. **Secondary on-call** — pulled in after 15 minutes without a clear mitigation path.
3. **Engineering lead** — engaged for any SEV-1, or if the incident is unresolved after 60 minutes.
4. **VP of Engineering** — engaged for SEV-1 incidents lasting longer than 2 hours or needing external communication.

## Rules of thumb

- Escalate early; nobody has ever regretted asking for help during a SEV-1.
- Escalation is a notification, not a request for permission.
- Always announce the escalation in the incident channel before you page.
